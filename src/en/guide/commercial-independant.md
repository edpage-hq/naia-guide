# Guide — Independent sales agent

Sell the products entrusted to you, follow your leads and your commissions.

## Your space

You work in the **Commercial space**, which gathers everything you need. Your menu contains:

- **Commercial space**: your network code and its status, your entrusted products, your leads and your contacts;
- **Leads**: your clients in progress, product by product;
- **Price grid**: your products' active offers and their current price;
- **Commissions**: your commissions and your monthly sheets;
- **My objectives and reviews**: your contract, your targets and your monthly reviews;
- **My training**: the trainings to pass;
- **My requests to edPage**: your questions to support.

You only see your own leads. Any other page takes you back to your space.

## Joining the network

1. Apply from the **Apply** link on the sign-in page ("Want to join the edPage network?"), choosing "Independent sales agent".
2. If your application is accepted, you receive a personal link by e-mail, valid for 7 days. There you confirm your address with a code, read your contract to the end, enter your details (you must be at least 18) and sign with a second code. You can change your code's name there.
3. Once edPage group has countersigned the contract, you create your account. Your code exists but it's **not active**, and no product is open yet.
4. Take your trainings from **My training** and pass their quiz (80% correct answers), within **10 days** of your account's creation. Your code then becomes active and your products open.

Remember to save your [payment details](./introduction#payment-details) in your profile.

## Your network code

Your code is in the form `ED-01TG4827`: your entry number in your country, the country code, then four digits. Naia assigns it and names you "Commercial Indépendant 01-Togo". It's private: you give it directly to your clients.

This number stays yours: if you become a referrer, only the prefix changes (`EDA-01TG4827`), and your clients stay linked to you. If Management records another country of practice for you, you receive a new code in that country.

- The first active code a client gives, at their first sign-up or first quote, is linked to them for good, on every product. A client has only one code.
- An ERA Admissions or ELEIS CRM sign-up with your code creates the lead and assigns it to you, if that product is entrusted to you.
- A client already linked to another code (network, referrer or partner) can't be linked to you. A client linked to a referrer or influence partner code is followed directly by edPage group.
- If a client forgot your code, Management can link it within 7 days of their sign-up, provided a contact with them is in your journal.

## Your leads

A lead follows a client on a product. Each product has its own steps:

| Product    | Steps                                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ERA, ELEIS | contacted, qualified, demo done, quote sent, signed                         |
| NEXTBOXX   | contacted, file submitted, interview, test, decision, first instalment paid |

"Lost" closes a lead, with its reason; a lost lead can be reopened.

- **Create a lead**: for your direct prospects, on your products. Naia tells you, for information, about an existing client with the same name, phone or e-mail. Enter the client's country (Togo by default).
- **Follow-up sheet**: qualification, discovery, demo, objections and next action. Change the step from the sheet's header.
- **Contact journal**: record each call, message, e-mail, meeting or visit. A contact is never edited or deleted.
- **Demo**: a demo lasts 60 days from the switch to "demo done". The sheet shows its end date, and you're warned 7 days before.

### Deadlines to meet

Naia monitors these deadlines (default values) and alerts you, and the sales manager, if one is passed:

- **first contact** on an ERA or ELEIS lead: 48 h, otherwise the lead goes to another sales agent;
- **answer to a NEXTBOXX candidate**: 24 business hours;
- **sending a proposal** after the client's request: 48 h;
- **follow-up of an ERA demo**: 7 days if the demo is active, 15 otherwise; **follow-up of an active ELEIS company**: 15 days;
- a lead with no contact for **60 days** goes back to the sales manager.

The leads list flags overdue ones and can be filtered on them.

## Proposals

From an ERA or ELEIS lead's sheet, draw up a **proposal**:

- pick active offers from the grid and their quantity. Prices aren't typed: each line takes the current price;
- choose the payment: in one go, or in 2 to 6 instalments;
- for an offer **on quote** (GED, ERA modules), request its price from the draft by describing the client's need. You never announce a price yourself;
- a draft can be edited and deleted; its PDF serves as a preview.

**Sending** is final: the proposal gets its number and a validity date (30 days by default), and the client receives a personal link to read it, sign the agreement if there is one, and pay. The lead switches to "quote sent". Once past its validity, a proposal has expired: draw up a new one.

::: warning Client outside the territory
For a client outside your country of practice (Togo by default), a proposal can only be sent after Management's written approval, given on the lead's sheet.
:::

### Requesting a discount

You never grant a discount alone. From the draft, request it with a justification:

- 5% at most, only on an ERA licence or an ELEIS offer, never on maintenance or NEXTBOXX;
- only one open request per proposal, and none on a proposal that already carries a partner code discount;
- the sales manager answers within 24 h. Until they decide, the proposal can't be sent. You can withdraw your request.

## Commissions

- You earn a commission on what your clients actually paid, line by line, at each payment: by default 25% on ERA and ELEIS licences, 15% on the ELEIS cloud subscription, and on each NEXTBOXX instalment according to the scale. Nothing on maintenance or options.
- Monthly payments are commissioned for the first 12 months, and complementary sales to the same client within 12 months of their first sale.
- A refund or a cancelled payment takes back the matching commission, deducted from your next sheet.
- On the 1st of each month, Naia draws up your **commission sheet** for the previous month, by product. Validate or contest it within 5 business days; without an answer, it's deemed validated. A validated sheet is paid within 5 business days, with its receipt.

## Contract, objectives and reviews

- Your contract starts with a **6-month trial period**, renewable only once for 6 months.
- Your **objectives** are set by month and by product: prospecting, demo and proposal activation, sales.
- At the end of each month, a **review** compares what you achieved with your targets. You find it in **My objectives and reviews**.
- 15 days before the end of the trial, the sales manager decides whether to renew your contract.
- At the end of the contract, your account is suspended, your code is withdrawn (it stays attached to your clients) and your open leads are reassigned. Licences collected within 6 months and monthly payments of subscriptions signed during your contract remain due to you, except for serious misconduct.
