# Guide — Influence partner

Publish your videos with your code and follow the clients it brings.

## Your space

You work in the **Ambassador space**. Your menu contains:

- **Ambassador space**: your code and its status, your personal links, the uses of your code and your videos;
- **Commissions**: your monthly statements;
- **My training**: the training to pass;
- **My requests to edPage**: your questions to support.

You never see clients' data: only the anonymous list of your code's uses (date, product, payment status, your share) and the month's counter.

## Joining the network

1. Apply from the **Apply** link on the sign-in page ("Want to join the edPage network?"), choosing "Influence partner".
2. If your application is accepted, you receive a personal link by e-mail, valid for 7 days. There you confirm your address with a code, read the charter to the end, choose your code, enter your details and sign with a second code.
3. Once edPage group has countersigned the charter, you create your account. Your code exists, but it's **not active**.
4. Take your training (about 1 h 30) from **My training** and pass its quiz (80% correct answers). Your code then becomes active.

Remember to save your [payment details](./introduction#payment-details) in your profile.

## Your partner code

Your code is in the form `EDI-NAME`. It's **public**: you share it in your videos.

- It gives the client a **5% discount** on their first purchase, once. This discount is reserved for clients edPage group has never known: Naia checks it, and refuses it to a known client ("This code is reserved for new clients").
- The first active code a client gives is linked to them for good. A client already linked to another code can't use yours, and you can't use your own code.
- Your space gives one **personal link** per product (ERA Admissions, ELEIS CRM, Kura pre-registration, sedver.com order) that pre-fills your code in the form.

## Your videos

Publish at least **3 videos a month** with the mention "Partner code EDI-…: I earn a commission".

In **My videos**, record each video (link and publication date): it's the required proof. Record it within 3 business days of publishing; after that, it's marked "late" but still counts. The month's counter tells you where you stand. If you start mid-month, the full minimum doesn't apply that month.

## Your commissions

- You earn a commission on the **first purchase** of each client who came through your code, calculated on what they actually paid: by default 15% on ERA and ELEIS licences, and 5% on each NEXTBOXX instalment of an admitted learner.
- A client whose discount is refused earns you nothing. If the refusal comes afterwards (check or fraud found), what's left of your share on that client is taken back on your next statement.
- On the 1st of each month, Naia draws up your **statement** for the previous month. Validate or contest it within 5 business days; without an answer, it's deemed validated, then paid with its receipt.

::: warning Fraud
At the second fraud found by Management on your code, it's suspended automatically.
:::
