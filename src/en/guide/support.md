# Need help?

## Where to ask

- **Built-in messaging** — from a project's page, this is the channel to use for anything related to that project: your message stays in context, with the rest of the team.
- **Support email** — for anything that isn't tied to a specific project (login issue, invitation not received…), contact your edPage group administrator.

## What we can help with

| Type of request   | Examples                                                                          |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Access / login    | Forgotten password, wrongly suspended account, blocking two-factor authentication |
| Billing / payment | Invoice not received, failed online payment, disputed amount                      |
| Usage             | Question about a feature, unexpected behavior                                     |
| Technical issue   | Blocking error, inaccessible page                                                 |

Depending on the nature of your request, expect a response anywhere from a few hours to one business day.

## Can't find your role in this guide?

Check the [introduction](./introduction) for the list of guides available by role, or contact whoever invited you to Naia (your project manager or your referrer) if you think you don't have the right level of access.
