# Guide — Training

Keep the training catalogue, sessions and validations.

This guide is for the **training manager** and the **trainer**, internal or external. The administrator, Management and the sales manager have the same rights in **Training**. Your **Today** page lists the trainings to validate.

## Catalogue and sessions

- Each training has a title, an audience (sales actors, partners and experts, clients, employees…), an optional product, a duration and a format.
- Create its **sessions**: dates, place or video link, trainer and number of seats.
- Upload the training's **materials**. They're versioned and readable by participants.

## Enrolments

- Enrol an account, or a participant without an account, in a session or individually.
- Sales actors are enrolled automatically in the trainings of their role and products.
- For the set-up session of an ERA or ELEIS client, attached to its deployment project, the client enrols their participants themselves from their customer area, within the number of seats.

## Attendance and validation

1. **Record attendance** of the participants.
2. **Validate** each participant or not. For independent sales agents, referrers and influence partners, the quiz is mandatory, with 80% correct answers.
3. A **certificate** of attendance or validation is issued on the letterhead.

Validation has effects elsewhere in Naia:

- a sales actor who has passed all their trainings gets their **code activated** and their products opened. The independent sales agent must do so within 10 days of their account's creation;
- an independent sales agent outside Togo can only declare an offline installation after passing a course marked **Habilitation: offline installation** (course setting);
- a partner can only join a first project after passing a "Partners and experts" training.

## New version of a training

When a product changes a lot, move the training to a new version: those who had passed it are enrolled again.

## Participant side

Each participant finds their trainings, sessions, materials and certificates in **My training**.
