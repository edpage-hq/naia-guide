# Introduction

Naia is **edPage group**'s project management platform: tracking phases and milestones, documents and deliverables, electronic signatures, invoicing and online payment, and built-in messaging, for everyone involved in a project — clients, internal team, external partners and referrers.

This guide is organized by role: each profile only has access to what concerns it, so each page covers exactly what you'll see in your own space.

## Roles on Naia

| Role                     | What they do on Naia                                                                 | Guide                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Client**               | Tracks their project's progress, approves deliverables, pays invoices                | [Client guide](./client)                                              |
| **Partner**              | Carries out tasks assigned by a project manager, submits invoices                    | [Partner guide](./partenaire)                                         |
| **Referrer**             | Tracks the portfolio of clients they've referred and their commissions               | [Referrer guide](./apporteur-affaires)                                |
| **Project manager**      | Runs one or more projects end to end                                                 | [Project manager & Collaborators guide](./chef-projet-collaborateurs) |
| **Collaborator**         | Carries out technical work (development, consulting, cybersecurity…)                 | [Project manager & Collaborators guide](./chef-projet-collaborateurs) |
| **Administrator**        | Manages accounts, billing and platform security                                      | [Administrator guide](./administrateur)                               |
| **Direction**            | Views an overview of the whole activity, decides on blocking for non-payment         | [Direction guide](./direction)                                        |
| **External contributor** | Member of a pole (Commercial, Support, HR, Billing) who sends their requests to Naia | [External contributor guide](./contributeur-externe)                  |

An account only ever has one role at a time — permissions and dashboard content adapt automatically.

## Signing in

Naia is invite-only: there's no self-service registration. You receive an invitation email with a link to activate your account and choose your password. Once your account is activated, sign in from the login page with your email address and that password.

If you haven't received an invitation but should have access to a project, contact whoever should have invited you (your project manager, or your referrer if you're a client) — see also the [Need help?](./support) page.

## Account security

### Two-factor authentication

If your role requires it (administrator, Management, sales manager, cashier, HR manager), Naia asks for a second code at each sign-in, after your password:

- **by default, a 6-digit code is e-mailed to you.** Enter it on the screen that appears. It is valid for 15 minutes; if it doesn't arrive, click **Send a new code** (at most once a minute) and check your spam folder;
- tick **Remember this device** to stop receiving codes on this browser for 30 days. Don't on a shared computer;
- if you prefer, turn on an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…) from **Settings > Security**: it then gives you the code, and no e-mail is sent anymore.

For other accounts, two-factor authentication is optional but recommended.

### Security settings

From **Settings > Security**, you can:

- change your password. Remembered devices are then forgotten, and a code will be asked again;
- turn on two-factor authentication with an app, or add a passkey;
- review your **remembered devices** and forget one, for example after using a computer that isn't yours;
- see your active sessions and sign out other devices.

## Notifications and messaging

You're notified automatically (bell icon at the top of the screen) at the key steps that concern you: milestone reached, deliverable submitted, document awaiting your approval, invoice due, delay reported. Every project also has built-in messaging to talk with the other people involved without leaving the platform.

## Language

Naia is available in French and English. The language switcher sits in the top-right corner of the screen, on every page.
