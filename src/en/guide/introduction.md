# Introduction

Naia is **edPage group**'s management tool. It brings together, in one place:

- **projects**: phases, milestones, tasks, documents, signatures and messaging;
- **sales**: the sales network's codes, leads, proposals, discounts and commissions;
- the **Cash** module: receipts, expenses, payment orders and statements;
- **support**, **human resources**, **payroll** and **training**;
- **Management** steering: decisions to take and indicators.

This guide is organized by role. Each account only sees what concerns it: each page describes what you'll see in your own space.

### Several roles on one account

A member of the edPage group team can hold several internal roles, for instance project manager and collaborator, or administrator and cashier. Their rights are then the sum of each role's rights, and their menu gathers all the matching sections.

External roles (client, partner, referrer, independent sales agent, influence partner, external contributor) stay alone on their account.

Some roles can't be held by the same person for the same operation: for instance, whoever validates an expense doesn't pay it. Naia tells you when an action is refused for that reason.

## Signing in

There is no open sign-up on Naia. Your account is opened in one of three ways:

- **by invitation**: you receive an e-mail with a link to activate your account and choose your password;
- **by the administrator**, who creates your account and gives you your credentials;
- **by application**, to join the sales network (independent sales agent, referrer or influence partner): on the sign-in page ("Want to join the edPage network?"), follow the **Apply** link and fill in the form. If your application is accepted, you receive a link to sign your document and create your account.

You then sign in from the sign-in page with your e-mail address and password, or with Google or Apple if your account is linked to them.

If you think you should have access to Naia without having received an invitation, contact the person who should have invited you, or see [Need help?](./support).

## Your "Today" page

After signing in, you land on **Today**. This page gathers:

- **To do**: what is waiting for an action from you, for instance your tasks in progress, expenses to approve, leaves to decide or tickets with no agent. Each line opens the screen where the action happens;
- **This month**: the few figures useful to your role;
- depending on your role, your recent projects and your billing due dates.

The menu on the left is grouped by area: Sales, Billing, Cash, Team, Support, Management and Administration. You only see the sections you have access to.

## Account security

### Two-factor authentication

If your role requires it (administrator, Management, sales manager, cashier, HR manager), Naia asks you for a second code at every sign-in, after your password:

- **by default, a 6-digit code is sent to you by e-mail.** Enter it on the screen that appears. It's valid for 15 minutes; if you don't receive it, click **Send a new code** (at most once a minute) and check your spam folder;
- tick **Remember this device for 30 days** to stop receiving a code on this browser for 30 days. Don't do it on a shared computer;
- if you prefer, turn on an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…) from **Settings > Security**: it gives you the code, and you no longer receive an e-mail.

For other accounts, two-factor authentication is optional but recommended: a reminder shows on your "Today" page until it's on.

### Security settings

From **Settings > Security**, you can:

- change your password. Remembered devices are then forgotten, and you'll be asked for a code again;
- turn on two-factor authentication with an app, or add a passkey;
- see your **remembered devices** and forget one, for instance after using a computer that isn't yours;
- see your active sessions and sign out other devices.

From **Settings > My activity**, you find the actions you've done on Naia and your sign-ins.

### Payment details

If edPage group pays you (partner, referrer, independent sales agent, influence partner), save your preferred payment method (mobile money or transfer) and your number or account in your profile. This information is encrypted; the cashier reads it when paying you.

## Notifications and messaging

You're notified (bell icon at the top of the screen, and often by e-mail) at the steps that concern you: milestone reached, document to approve, invoice due, request to decide, deadline passed. Each project also has built-in messaging to talk with the other participants without leaving Naia.

## Language

Naia is available in French and English. The language selector is at the top right of the screen, on every page.
