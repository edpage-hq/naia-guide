# Guide — Human resources

Keep staff files, leaves and payroll; view your own file.

This guide covers two profiles: the **HR manager**, who keeps the module, and the **employee**, who views their file.

## Part A — HR manager

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for you (see [Account security](./introduction#two-factor-authentication)). Only you and Management see salaries; the administrator manages accounts but has no access to files.

### Staff files

In **Human resources**, **Staff** tab:

- open a file for an internal account (never for a client, a partner or a sales actor): position, contract, line manager, salary, payment details;
- attach documents: contract, ID, diploma, medical… They're stored encrypted and readable by you, Management and the employee concerned;
- the end of a trial period or a fixed-term contract is flagged to you 30 days before (by default).

Each file opening and each document read is recorded in the audit log. After an employee leaves and the retention period ends (5 years by default), you can purge their file: documents and personal data are erased, identity and dates kept.

### Leaves and absences

- **Settings** tab: define leave types (days per year, paid or not, counted against the balance or not) and public holidays.
- **Leaves** tab: the requests and the calendar of absences for the next two months. A request is validated by the employee's line manager; failing that, by you or Management. You can cancel a leave already validated.
- Leave is counted in business days, without weekends or public holidays.

### Account requests

For an arrival or a departure, send an **account creation or removal request** to the administrator, who handles it.

### Payroll

In **Payroll**, each month:

1. **Open the month**: each employee in post with a base salary gets a payslip.
2. **Enter the variables**: bonuses, overtime, deductions. Naia calculates gross pay, CNSS, IRPP and net pay, with the rates in force.
3. **Send the batch** to Management, who validates it or sends it back with a reason. You never validate a batch you prepared.

On validation, payslips (`BUL-AAAA-XXX`) are issued on the letterhead, each payslip gets its payment order in the Cash module, and social charges and IRPP become expenses to pay the following month.

Naia also produces the returns to file in Excel (monthly CNSS and IRPP, annual statement) and the employment certificate. E-filing happens outside Naia. For an employee who joins mid-year, enter their opening payroll totals.

## Part B — Employee {#part-b-employee}

**My HR file** gives you, and you only, access to:

- your file and your documents;
- your **leaves**: your balance, your requests, and a form to make a new one. You can cancel a request while it's pending;
- your **payslips** and their payments.

If you're a line manager, your team's leaves to decide appear on your **Today** page.
