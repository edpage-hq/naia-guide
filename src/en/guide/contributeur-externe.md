# Guide — External contributor

Send your pole's requests to Naia.

## Welcome to Naia

You belong to one of edPage group's poles — Commercial, Support, HR or Billing — and your account lets you send your requests straight to Naia, instead of by e-mail or phone.

Your access is deliberately narrow: you only see **My space**, which holds your pole's form and the list of your submissions. You don't have access to projects, documents or messaging. Searches (client, project, collaborator) only show a reference and a name.

## Getting your account

Your account is created by Naia's administrator, who gives you your credentials. On your first sign-in, change your password from **Settings > Security** (the gear icon at the top of the screen). If you leave the pole, the administrator revokes your access.

## Following your submissions

The **My submissions** list, visible to you only, shows each submission with its status:

- **Sent**: Naia has received your submission; it's waiting to be handled;
- **Processed**: its effect exists in Naia — the project is created, the task is added, the collaborator is assigned, or the payment status has been taken into account.

There's nothing else for you to do: a submission switches to "Processed" on its own.

## Commercial pole

- **New project**: for a client who has just signed, search for them or type their name if they're new, pick the project type, then **Create the project**. This is a request: an administrator validates it and appoints the project manager. The project then gets its reference (e.g. `NAIA-0142`).
- **Report a development need**: same form as the Support pole (below). The task created carries the **Commercial** badge.

## Support pole

**Development need**: when a ticket reveals a need for the development team:

1. enter the ticket reference if you have one (e.g. `TCK-4821`) — it's optional;
2. pick the project concerned, or **Unknown project** if you don't know which project it is;
3. describe the need, then **Send to Naia**.

On a known project, the need immediately becomes a task on the project, with the **Support** badge, and the project manager is notified. On an unknown project, an administrator picks the right project before creating the task.

## HR pole

The **Pending reinforcement needs** list gathers project managers' reinforcement requests, with the required profile. For each one, pick the collaborator chosen among existing accounts, then **Confirm the assignment**: they automatically join the project team, and the project manager is notified.

A confirmed line disappears for every HR account: two people in the pole can't handle the same need. If the person chosen doesn't have a Naia account yet (an external expert, for instance), ask the administrator to create it first.

You're notified, in the app and by e-mail, of every new reinforcement need.

## Billing pole

The **Signalled billable milestones** list shows each billable milestone validated on a project, with the amount Naia signalled. For each one, set the **payment status**, then **Update the status**:

- **Up to date**: the payment is settled; the milestone leaves your list;
- **Overdue**: Direction and the administrator are alerted and decide whether to block the project. The milestone stays in your list: once the payment is settled, set it back to **Up to date** so the project can be unblocked.

You're notified, in the app and by e-mail, of every new billable milestone. No accounting data is stored in Naia: you only record a status there.
