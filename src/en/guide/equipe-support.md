# Guide — Support team

Handle clients' requests and set up the products sold.

This guide is for three roles: the **support agent**, the **deployment officer** and the **NEXTBOXX team**. Your **Today** page lists tickets with no agent and your tickets in progress.

## Where tickets come from

Everything becomes a ticket in **Support**:

- Naia's form (**My requests to edPage** for sales actors);
- the <support@edpage.net> mailbox. A reply to a support e-mail adds to the original ticket;
- product clients' customer area.

Tickets are sorted into **queues**:

- **Clients**;
- **Network**: independent sales agents, referrers and influence partners, with an answer within 24 business hours;
- **NEXTBOXX**: teaching questions and admission decisions, routed to the NEXTBOXX team.

## Handling a ticket

On a ticket's page, the conversation is on the left, and the **Follow-up** panel on the right.

1. **Take it on**: choose the agent, then the status, the level (N1 to N3) and the category (incident, request, question, product bug, visit, teaching, appeal against a refused discount).
2. **Check the support entitlement**, shown on the ticket. It follows the client's payment: licence and maintenance up to date, subscription up to date, full support during a demo, community only for the free ELEIS CRM. SEDVER clients are served in WHMCS, except direct clients.
3. **Reply** to the client, or add an **internal note** they don't see.
4. **Resolve**: the client can rate the answer from 1 to 5 in their customer area.

### Deadlines

Depending on severity, 4 h, 24 h or 72 h for an ELEIS licence; 5 business days for an evaluation visit; 24 business hours for the rest. You're alerted before the deadline passes, Management when it does. A requalified ticket recalculates its deadline.

## Customer area

From the ticket, send the client a **personal link** to their edPage customer area. Without an account, they create and follow their requests there, find their proposals and receipts, and give or withdraw their **consent to a remote intervention**: a consent covers one ticket, for a limited time, and it's traced.

## Evaluation visits

Plan a visit from the ticket, then write its report and costing, travel costs included.

## Escalation and appeals

- **Escalate to a project**: a product bug that needs development becomes a task on the chosen project, with the **Support** badge.
- **Appeal against a refused discount**: Management decides. If granted, the discount applies to the proposal still in draft.

## Deployment projects

The first paid sale of ERA or ELEIS opens a **set-up** or onboarding **project**. It's given to the deployment officer, who runs it like an ordinary project (see the [Project manager guide](./chef-projet-collaborateurs)). Nothing is invoiced on it: it's included in the sale. The client's user training sessions are attached to it, and the client enrols their participants from their customer area.
