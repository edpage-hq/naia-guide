# Guide — Project manager & Collaborators

Run and execute projects day to day.

This guide covers two closely related profiles: the **Project manager**, who runs a project end to end, and **Collaborators / Consultants** (7 job types), who carry out the technical work assigned to them.

## Part A — Project manager

### Creating and running a project

- Create a project from **Projects > New project**: client, project manager and **project type**. The chosen type pre-fills the phases and milestones of the matching methodology (eight types: Website / digital presence, Custom application or platform, Digital transformation consulting, Information systems architecture, Cloud & infrastructure, Cybersecurity, AI & data, Training).
- A project can also come from a Commercial pole request validated by the administrator, or, for a **deployment project**, from the first paid sale of ERA or ELEIS (it's then given to the deployment officer, and nothing is invoiced on it).
- Adjust the pre-filled phases and milestones if needed (rename, reorder, set each billable milestone's billing percentage) from the project page's **Tracking** tab.
- Each project carries a unique reference (e.g. `NAIA-0142`), which the poles use to refer to it.
- Build the team from the **Team** tab and assign tasks, including to external partners. Specify how they're paid (flat fee, milestone percentage or daily rate). A partner can only join their first project after passing a "Partners and experts" training.
- From **Invite**, invite a partner or a collaborator to Naia.
- Need reinforcement? In the **Team** tab, **Request reinforcement**: describe the required profile (e.g. "Senior Vue developer") and add details if needed. The request goes to the HR pole; the person they pick joins the team, and you're notified.
- The Support and Commercial poles can report a development need on your project: it becomes a task, with a **Support** or **Commercial** badge showing its origin, and you're notified.
- Track overall progress with the **Gantt** view (by phase and milestone) or the **Kanban** view (by task status).

### Task due dates and priorities

- When you add or edit a task, give it a **due date** (optional) and a **priority**: low, normal, high or urgent. It's normal by default. Only you, as project manager, and the administrator can change them: the assignee sees them but can't edit them.
- Each task's badges show its priority and due date: **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Due tomorrow** or the date. In the **Tasks** tab, filter by priority or by due date (overdue, within 2 days, no due date), in the list as in the Kanban.
- If a task is due before a task it depends on, a warning shows under the task. Nothing is blocked: it's up to you to adjust the dates.
- The day before the due date, the assignee gets a reminder. The day after the due date, if the task isn't done, you and the assignee are told it's overdue, once. Moving the due date or finishing the task closes the alert. A project blocked for non-payment raises no reminder and no alert.
- On your **Today** page, the **Tasks of my projects coming due** block lists your projects' tasks that are overdue or due within 2 days.

### Milestones and billing

- When you validate a **non-billable** milestone, add a report visible to the client and, when useful, a demo link.
- Validating a **billable** milestone triggers its invoice: the Cash module issues it itself, with the milestone's percentage, and sends it to the client. The first billable milestone gives the deposit, the last one the balance.
- An overdue invoice is flagged to you for information. Only an overdue payment reported by the Billing pole can lead to the project being blocked, on the decision of Management or the administrator: you can neither block nor unblock a project.
- On a project **blocked for non-payment**, tasks already started can finish, but no one can start a new task or validate a milestone until it's unblocked. You can still plan, talk and upload documents.

### Partner invoices

When a partner uploads their invoice in the **Partner payments** tab, check it and validate it. Validation creates a payment order in the Cash module; the invoice switches to "paid" on its own once the cashier has paid the order. The partner can withdraw their invoice until it's validated; after that, it's the payment order that gets cancelled.

### Reports, signatures and partner contracts

- In the **Reports** tab, create a report from one of the 12 templates (RA-01 to RA-04, RP-01 to RP-08): the sections are pre-filled, you complete them, then send it for signature. The client and edPage sign with a code; each document gets a number (RAI for an RA, RAP for an RP).
- Project documents are numbered PF (pro forma), AVT (amendment), ATT (certificate, created when the project closes); new projects are PRJ-YYYY-NNN. Numbers already issued are kept.
- Before adding a partner to a project, they must have signed the **framework contract (CT-01)** and the **confidentiality agreement (CT-02)**: the **Team** tab offers to send them. To give them tasks, send their **mission sheet (CT-03)** (scope, dates, expenses, conditions) and wait for their signature. The refusal message always names the missing document.

### Amendments and closing

- Request an amendment from the **Amendments** tab if the project's scope, timeline or budget must change. The administrator prepares the document, which the client signs; the budget impact only applies once the amendment is signed.
- When closing the project, tick its possible **NextBoxx** eligibility and add a free note if the project can serve as a real case for trainings.

## Part B — Collaborators / Consultants

The flow is the same whatever your job (Digital transformation consultant, Developer, IS architect, Cloud & infrastructure expert, Cybersecurity expert, AI & data expert, Trainer): only the expected deliverable changes.

### Your assignments

- View the tasks assigned to you, across all projects, from **My Tasks** or a project's **Tasks** tab.
- Update their progress (to do, in progress, done) and upload your deliverables from their page.
- **My Tasks** puts overdue tasks first, then sorts them by due date and priority. The project manager sets the due date and priority. You get a reminder the day before each due date, and an alert if it passes.
- Talk with the project manager and, depending on the context, with the client, through the project's messaging.

### Your file and your trainings

If you're an edPage group employee, **My HR file** gives you access to your file, your leaves and your payslips (see the [Human resources guide](./ressources-humaines#part-b-employee)). **My training** lists the trainings you're enrolled in.
