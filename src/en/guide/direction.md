# Guide — Direction

A consolidated view of the activity, across every portfolio.

## Welcome to Naia

Your Naia space gives you a **read-only**, consolidated view of every one of edPage group's projects and financial indicators.

## Consolidated project view

- Access a view of every ongoing project, across all project managers and portfolios.
- Spot at-risk projects (delay, non-payment, suspension) at a glance, without having to open each project's page individually.

## Financial indicators

- View the global financial indicators: invoiced and collected revenue, pending or overdue invoices, commissions due and paid to referrers.
- Track each referrer's performance (number of clients referred, revenue generated, commissions due and paid).

## The scope of your access

Your access is deliberately limited to viewing: you have access to neither account and role management, nor security settings, nor project messaging — those remain reserved for the Administrator and the Project manager.
