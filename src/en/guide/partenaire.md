# Guide — Partner

Carry out your tasks and track your compensation.

## Welcome to Naia

You've been invited by a project manager to work on one or more projects, with access limited to the tasks that concern you — you don't see the whole project the way a client or project manager would.

## Your tasks

Your dashboard and each project's **Tasks** tab bring together the work assigned to you:

- view the tasks and specifications that concern you, across every project;
- update their progress (to do, in progress, done) and upload your deliverables directly from their page.

## Your compensation

The terms of your compensation (flat fee, percentage of a milestone, or daily rate) are agreed with the project manager for each project — refer to your service agreement for the details.

- Submit your own invoice for the agreed compensation from the **Partner payments** tab of the relevant project.
- Track your payment status (due or paid) directly from that tab — the administrator records the payment once it's been made.

## Communication

Talk with the internal team via built-in messaging or task comments. You're notified of approvals, feedback, or new deadlines.

::: warning Suspended project
If a project is suspended because of an unpaid client invoice, your access to the affected tasks is temporarily blocked, until the project manager or the administrator lifts the suspension.
:::
