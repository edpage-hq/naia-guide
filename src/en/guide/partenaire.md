# Guide — Partner

Carry out your tasks and follow your pay.

## Welcome to Naia

A project manager has invited you to work on one or more projects. Your access is limited to the tasks that concern you: you don't see the whole project the way the client or the project manager does.

::: info Training before your first project
Before joining a first project, you must have passed a training from the "Partners and experts" track. You're enrolled from **My training**. A partner already assigned to a project before this rule can still be assigned.
:::

## Your tasks

Your **Today** page and **My Tasks** gather the assignments given to you, across all projects:

- view the tasks and specifications that concern you;
- update their progress (to do, in progress, done) and upload your deliverables from their page.

## Your pay

How you're paid (flat fee, percentage of a milestone or daily rate) is agreed with the project manager for each project. See your service contract for the details.

1. Upload your invoice for the agreed pay from the project's **Partner payments** tab.
2. The project manager (or the administrator) validates it. This validation creates a payment order in edPage group's Cash module. Until it's validated, you can withdraw your invoice.
3. After a review period (48 h by default), the cashier pays you with the method saved in your profile.
4. Your invoice switches to "paid" on its own. You receive a **payment receipt** by e-mail, which you can also download.

Remember to save your [payment details](./introduction#payment-details) in your profile.

## Communication

Talk with the internal team through the built-in messaging or task comments. You're notified of validations, feedback and new deadlines.

::: warning Project blocked for non-payment
If a project is blocked for non-payment on the client's side, you're notified. You can finish a task already started, but not start a new one until it's unblocked. You keep access to the project, your documents and messaging.
:::
