# Guide — Sales manager

Run the sales network: leads, codes, discounts, applications, contracts and commissions.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for you (see [Account security](./introduction#two-factor-authentication)). Your **Today** page lists what's waiting for you: leads to assign, applications to review, discounts to decide, contested commission sheets.

## Leads

In **Leads**, you see and work all leads, for every product.

- **Assign**: a lead with no code is assigned according to the "Assignment of leads without a code" setting (balanced distribution, or waiting for a manual assignment). A lead left without a sales agent is flagged to you; assign or reassign it from its sheet, with a reason.
- **Leads linked to a referrer or influence partner**: they never go to an independent sales agent. They stay in your pool: edPage group sells, and the commission goes to the code's holder.
- **Leads that come back to you**: an open lead with no contact for 60 days, and the leads of a sales agent whose product is withdrawn or whose contract has ended.
- **Deadlines**: you're alerted of each deadline passed by a sales agent (first contact, proposal, follow-up). The list can be filtered on overdue leads.

You can draw up a proposal yourself from a lead. A lead you follow needs no territory approval.

## Codes and videos

- **Codes**: manually activate the code of an actor whose training is validated, suspend or reactivate it, each time with a reason.
- **Partner videos**: follow, month by month, each influence partner's number of videos and late proofs. A shortfall raises an alert; suspending the code remains your decision.

## Discounts to decide

Sales agents request their discounts in Naia; you decide, from **Discounts to decide**. You're told of each request, and you have **24 h** to answer.

- Validate: the proposal is recalculated and its PDF shows "discount granted".
- Refuse: a reason is required, and the author sees it.
- You never decide on a request you made yourself.

## Applications and contracts

- **Applications**: the applications submitted from the "Join the edPage network" page. Accept one by confirming or correcting the role and choosing the entrusted products: the applicant receives their signing link. Or decline it with an internal reason; the applicant receives a neutral e-mail, without the reason.
- **Commercial contracts**: start the contract of a sales agent, referrer or influence partner, adjust their monthly objectives, read their reviews. 15 days before the end of a sales agent's trial period, you're asked to decide: renew once, or don't renew. You can also end a contract early, with 15 days' notice or immediately for serious misconduct.

## Sales commissions

**Sales commissions** shows all beneficiaries' commissions and their monthly sheets. When a beneficiary contests their sheet, you settle it.

## Training

You can be the trainer of a session and validate sales actors' trainings (see the [Training guide](./formation)).
